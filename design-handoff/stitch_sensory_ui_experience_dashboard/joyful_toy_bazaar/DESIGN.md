## Brand & Style
The design system embraces the joy, wonder, and nostalgia of childhood play, anchored directly in the traditional spinning top (*latim*) emblem. Designed for parents, gift-givers, and young hobbyists, the UI communicates unhurried delight, trustworthy commerce, and warm craftsmanship.

The visual style blends **Modern Playful Minimalism** with **Warm Tactile Charm**:
- **Bubbly & Friendly:** Generously rounded pill shapes, friendly typography, and soft pill badges that evoke safe, child-friendly toy designs.
- **Warm Canvas:** Rather than clinical cool grays, interfaces rest on creamy, sun-warmed cloud whites (`#FFFBF9` / `#FDF7F4`) to evoke organic card stock and premium packaging.
- **Festive Rhythm:** Vibrant red and honey gold brand anchors are balanced by crisp royal blue accents to maintain high legibility and delightful visual cadence across shopping categories.

## Colors
The palette extracts direct heritage equity from the brand mark, balancing energetic primary warmth with clean digital clarity:

- **Primary Red (`#D71921` / `#E11D24`):** The signature emblem red. Used for header banners, core call-to-actions, cart counts, sale alerts, and primary branding frames.
- **Secondary Amber Gold (`#FDB813`):** Sourced from the spinning top and hand illustration. Drives ratings, promotional badges, loyalty ribbons, and joyful highlighting containers.
- **Joy Accent Blue (`#1E5BFF`):** A bright complementary counterweight. Reserved for trust seals, technical toy specifications, filter highlights, and informational feedback badges.
- **Warm Surfaces:** Surface base rests on `#FFFBF9`, cards and product panels elevate in pure `#FFFFFF`, and container dividers use subtle warm bisque tones (`#F5EBE6`).
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text is grounded in deep warm charcoal `#271E1C` (not cold black), ensuring readable, accessible typography that maintains the cozy boutique voice.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers geometric clarity infused with friendly, welcoming terminal curvatures. 

- **Headlines:** Set in bold and extrabold weights (`700` and `800`) with subtle negative tracking. This captures the bubbly, spirited atmosphere of a toy store without sacrificing digital professionalism.
- **Product Names & Pricing:** Displayed in clean semi-bold weights, with prices given bold hierarchy alongside the national currency symbol.
- **Badges & Microcopy:** Rendered with uppercase or title-case pill tags using `label-md` and `label-sm`, keeping technical details like age ranges ("3-6 Yrs"), battery warnings, and authentic craft tags crisp and readable.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 12-column layout on desktop transitions smoothly to a 4-column structure on mobile devices, ensuring product grids, deal banners, and category bubbles stay roomy and tap-friendly:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and a centered maximum container width of `1280px`. Product cards sit comfortably in 3 or 4-column groupings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px canvas margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with compact 12px gutters and 16px lateral padding, allowing 2-up toy cards to display with full clarity and large thumb-friendly touch areas (minimum 44px height).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the system is buoyant and warm, avoiding harsh black drops in favor of golden-amber and terracotta tinted diffusions:

- **Resting Product Cards:** Layer 1 elevation with a soft, warm ambient drop (`0 4px 14px -2px rgba(215, 25, 33, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`) over a 1px soft border (`#F3E9E4`).
- **Interactive Hover:** Elements rise smoothly with `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(215, 25, 33, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(253, 184, 19, 0.16)` and a subtle `transform: translateY(-3px)`.
- **Modals, Drawers & Floating Cart Bar:** Layer 3 depth using a wide diffused shadow (`0 20px 32px -8px rgba(39, 30, 28, 0.14)`) combined with a backdrop blur of `8px` on scrim overlays.

## Shapes
A pill-shaped, soft curvature philosophy (`roundedness: 3`) reinforces safe, playful toy aesthetics:

- **Chips, Category Avatars & Badges:** Full-pill radiuses (`9999px`) creating candy-like interactive objects.
- **Buttons:** Smooth pill contours (`9999px` or minimum `24px` radius) providing high tactile appeal.
- **Cards & Modals:** Generous `1.5rem` (`24px`) to `2rem` (`32px`) corner radiuses that mirror molded wooden toys and friendly packaging containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Pill-shaped, rich crimson red (`#D71921`) background with pure white text, accompanied by an optional spinning toy or shopping bag icon. Hover state introduces a warm scale shift (`1.02`) and amber glow.
- **Secondary Action (Wishlist / Quick View):** Warm cloud base (`#FFFBF9`) with a 1.5px crimson or amber outline and colored typography.
- **Accent Pop (Flash Deals / Gamified CTA):** Bright Amber Gold (`#FDB813`) surface with deep charcoal text (`#271E1C`) for maximum shelf urgency.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Category Nav Chips:** Rounded pill containers with a miniature circular icon (e.g., Latim / Top, Building Blocks, Dolls, Board Games). Active chip glows in red with white text; inactive sits on `#FFFBF9` with soft outline.
- **Offer & Age Tags:** Micro-pills pinned to card corners. Amber Yellow (`#FDB813`) for discounts ("20% OFF"), Joy Blue (`#1E5BFF`) for developmental attributes ("Ages 3+"), and Crimson Red (`#D71921`) for "Authentic Craft" & "Hot Seller".

### Product Cards
- Elevated white containers resting on warm cream backgrounds.
- High-aspect ratio image viewport with curved inner masks (`16px`).
- Bottom zone features bold localized currency, star rating with amber top accent, and a quick one-tap circular "+" button in primary red.

### Input Fields & Search Bar
- Search bars are fully pill-shaped with an integrated prominent red search button on the right edge.
- Inputs feature warm off-white fills (`#FFFDFB`), soft inner borders, and transition to a 2px royal blue focus ring for accessible keyboard navigation.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Distinctively rounded checkboxes (6px corner radius) and circular radios that fill with primary red or accent blue when checked, featuring clean white check icons.
